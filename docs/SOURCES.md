# Sources and interpretation policy

The bundled chapters contain the classical Chinese text in simplified display form and James Legge's 1891 English translation. Legge's translation is public domain. The English translation is preserved as a historical source; it is not presented as the only correct interpretation.

Reflection prompts are original editorial prompts released with this project. They are intentionally open-ended. A prompt is not therapy, medical advice, legal advice, or a prediction. For crisis or safety concerns, users should contact an appropriate local professional or emergency service.

When building an application on top of this server, show the source and distinguish a quotation from an application's own paraphrase. Do not attribute a generated interpretation to Lao Tzu.
